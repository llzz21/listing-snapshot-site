Privacy Policy

Listing Snapshot for Etsy · Last updated: 2026-11-21

Listing Snapshot does not collect, transmit, or store any of your data on any server. There is no backend. Everything the extension does happens inside your own browser tab.

What the extension reads

  • Only the product cards that are already rendered on a www.etsy.com page you have opened — the title, the price as written on the page, rating, review count, the listing link, the thumbnail address, the shop name, and any visible sold/favourite signal.
  • It reads the page you are already looking at once, when you click the toolbar icon. It does not browse, does not paginate, and does not visit any page on your behalf.

What it never touches

  • Cookies, session tokens, passwords, auth storage, or any sign-in state.
  • Your browsing history, your other tabs, or any website other than www.etsy.com.
  • Any form of writing to Etsy: it never clicks, submits, posts, or buys.

Where your data is stored

CSV and TSV exports are generated in the page and delivered straight to you; they are never sent anywhere. Snapshots are written only to your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local) on your own device, capped at the 20 most recent, and they are deleted the moment you delete them or clear your browser storage. They never leave your machine and cannot be read by anyone else.

Analytics and third-party code

None. The extension contains no analytics script, no crash reporter, no tracking pixel, no third-party SDK, and no remotely hosted code. All of its logic ships inside the downloaded package. Permissions requested are limited to storage and access to https://www.etsy.com/*.

Children and payment

This extension is a business tool for Etsy sellers and is not directed at children. Version 1 has no payment, no subscription, and no account of any kind.
